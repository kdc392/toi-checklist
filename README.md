# TOI Site Start Checklist

A checklist for the start of an installation. Fill it out on your phone, make a PDF report, and email it.

**App link:** https://kdc392.github.io/toi-checklist/

Open the link, then follow the steps for your phone below. Do this once. After that, open the app from its icon on your Home Screen.

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the app link in **Safari**. Use a regular tab, not a Private tab, and not a link opened inside another app.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**. If you don't see it, tap **View More** first.
4. Leave **Open as Web App** switched on if you see it, then tap **Add**.
5. Open the app from the new **TOI Checklist** icon, not from Safari.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** at the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen** if you don't see that).
4. Tap **Install**, then open the app from its icon.

## Before you go to the job site

- Open the app from its icon **once while you have signal**. This saves it to your phone so it works without signal afterward.
- Use the Home Screen icon every time. Entries and photos are saved on your phone, and they are safest when you use the installed app.

## Using the app on site

- Your answers and photos are saved on your phone as you go, but they stay on that one phone.
- **Take new photos with the camera.** Picking old photos from your library may fail without signal.
- Tap **Create PDF report** when you finish, then **Email / share PDF** once you have signal and send it right away. The emailed PDF is the official record. Don't rely on the phone to store it.

## Updates

The app updates itself. If a change doesn't show up, open the app with signal, close it completely, and open it again.
